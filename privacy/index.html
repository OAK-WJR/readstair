ReadStair Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

ReadStair marks the words you don’t know on photos of printed book pages and in e-books you open. It is built to work without collecting anything about you:

  • There is no ReadStair server and no account.
  • There are no analytics, ads or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • ReadStair makes no network requests of its own. The only data it sends anywhere is your word list and vocabulary level, and only to your own iCloud, as described below.
  • Because the developer never receives your data, it is never sold or shared.

If you turn on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give the developer crash reports and usage statistics. iOS controls this, not ReadStair.

What ReadStair keeps on your device

  • Photos of the book pages you scan or pick, and small thumbnails of them
  • The text recognized on those pages
  • Pages of e-books you open (EPUB or plain text files): ReadStair reads the file once, lays out its pages on your device and keeps those page images and their text. It does not keep or upload the file.
  • Book titles and where you stopped reading
  • Your word list: the words you looked up or marked, whether you know each one or are still learning it, and the sentence where you first met it
  • Your vocabulary level and the language you chose for meanings

This data stays in ReadStair’s own storage on your device. Like other app data, it is included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you make them.

What goes to your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for ReadStair, ReadStair keeps your word list and vocabulary level in sync across your devices through your private iCloud database (Apple’s CloudKit). For each word this is the word itself, whether you know it or are learning it, whether that came from looking it up or from its word card, how common it is (its frequency band in ReadStair’s word list), when it last changed, and the sentence where you first met it. Apart from that one sentence per word, page photos, e-book pages, the rest of the recognized text and book titles are never uploaded.

Your private iCloud database is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and counts toward your iCloud storage. Only you can access it; the developer cannot see it. Apple’s Privacy Policy covers how Apple handles iCloud data.

Apple features that run on your device

ReadStair uses frameworks built into iOS, and they run on your device:

  • The document scanner captures pages, and text recognition reads the words on them.
  • Translation shows meanings in your language. iOS downloads translation languages from Apple when needed; iOS handles that download, not ReadStair.
  • Where Apple Intelligence is available and turned on, it explains a word in its sentence using the on-device model.
  • Pronunciation uses the voices built into iOS.

Camera, photos and files

  • Camera: ReadStair asks for camera access the first time you scan pages and uses the camera only while you scan. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.
  • Photos: when you add pages from Photos, iOS shows its own photo picker and ReadStair receives only the photos you pick. ReadStair does not ask for access to your photo library and never changes it.
  • Files: when you open an e-book, iOS shows its own file picker and ReadStair can read only the file you pick. If that file is in iCloud Drive and not yet on your device, iOS downloads it first.
  • Find Free Books opens the Standard Ebooks website in your web browser. ReadStair sends nothing to it; what you do there is between you and that website.

ReadStair asks for no other permissions.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a book in ReadStair to remove its page photos or e-book pages and their text. Words you added while reading it stay in your word list, together with the sentence where you first met them.
  • Delete the app to remove everything ReadStair stored on the device. Copies in earlier device backups remain until those backups are deleted.
  • To delete ReadStair’s data from iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, open your iCloud storage, choose ReadStair and delete its data (the labels vary a little between iOS versions). ReadStair then clears the word list and level on your devices as well; your books stay.

Children

ReadStair collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/OAK-WJR/readstair/issues.
