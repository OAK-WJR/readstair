ReadStair support

ReadStair is a free iPhone and iPad app for reading real English books. Scan a printed page, or open an EPUB, a PDF or a plain text file, and ReadStair underlines only the words you may not know; tap one to see what it means.

Questions, problems or ideas:

  • Email: oak.wjr@gmail.com
  • Or open an issue: https://github.com/OAK-WJR/readstair/issues

A parent or guardian can ask for a book their child published on readstair.com to be removed: email oak.wjr@gmail.com with the book’s link.

For parents: sharing an age range

Until iOS shares an age range, Library lists ReadStair’s books for ages 4+ and 9+ only, plus whatever is in a folder you add yourself, and Project Gutenberg isn’t offered. To see books for ages 13 and up, or to publish a book, a child needs to share an age range with ReadStair. ReadStair uses it on the device, to choose which books to show and whether Publish is offered, and never sends the age range to anyone.

On a parent’s iPhone or iPad: Settings > Family > the child’s name > Apple Account > Age Range for Apps > Share. Then, in ReadStair, tap Library at the top and choose Share Age Range.

If you declined when iOS asked: Settings > your name > Personal Information > Age Range for Apps.
