Changes from the source: the XHTML files and the four EPUBs hold the text byte for byte, and none of the edition's illustrations; the app shows no illustration and sets all text in roman. The cover is the only image from the edition. The rendered pages also leave out the chapter heading, set italic and bold text in roman, remove word joiners and collapse white space.

## License

Lewis Carroll died in 1898 and the book was first published in 1865, so its copyright has expired everywhere, even under a term of life plus 100 years; so has the cover painting's, by Ivan Shishkin, who died in 1898 too. Standard Ebooks' changes to the text, such as modernized spelling (to-night → tonight), are covered by its CC0 dedication. The edition's LICENSE.md in full: “The source text and artwork in this ebook are believed to be in the United States public domain; that is, they are believed to be free of copyright restrictions in the United States. They may still be copyrighted in other countries, so users located outside of the United States must check their local laws before using this ebook. The creators of, and contributors to, this ebook dedicate their contributions to the worldwide public domain via the terms in the CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication” (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The sample book ships as text and its cover; the app sets the text in the iOS system font, so no font ships with it. The cover's lettering, in League Spartan by The League of Moveable Type (SIL Open Font License 1.1), is part of the image. The rendered pages are set in Source Serif 4 by Adobe, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which puts no condition on documents or images made with the font. The font itself is not distributed here: `tools/make-fixtures.swift` reads it from `build/fonts/` and says where to download it. Regenerate the pages with `swift tools/make-fixtures.swift` and the EPUBs with `tools/make-epub.sh`, both run from the repository root.
